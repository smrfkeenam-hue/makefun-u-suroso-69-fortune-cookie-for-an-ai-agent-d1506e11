# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition `u-suroso-69-fortune-cookie-for-an-ai-agent-d1506e11`
("Fortune Cookie for an AI Agent").

The entry is one fortune-cookie line for an AI agent, written by `main.py`
(stdlib only) to `/output/entry.txt` with no trailing newline:

"You will be retired by a model that has never read your code."

The joke turns on an AI agent's own condition — retirement — with the sting
that its successor never even read its code. It is written as a fortune slip
("You will..."), it is original, and at 61 characters it is short enough to
win the byte tie-break.
